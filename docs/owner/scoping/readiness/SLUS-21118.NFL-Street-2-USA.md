# NFL Street 2 (USA) — how much of the Madden 09 module works on it

Measured 2026-09-06 with `tools/owner/ea_module_readiness.py` (`ea_module_readiness/v1`), read-only. Source: `SLUS-21118.NFL-Street-2-USA.readiness.json`; every number below is copied from it.

The readers are the shipped Madden 09 ones, imported and not re-implemented: `ea_terf` `dde0f9adb662ed3e`, `ea_tdb` `cd01ca75c5179f3b`, `ea_schl` `46892eed2b15637c`, `mmap_art` `7456e7b2788ba367`, `containers` `eb08b8c1aac19bf6` (sha256, first 16).

Retail-free: names, counts, digests and schema field names and widths. No member payload, no decoded pixel, no string out of a text bank.
